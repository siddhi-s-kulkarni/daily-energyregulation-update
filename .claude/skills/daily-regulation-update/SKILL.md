## Scope

- Energy-sector regulation and policy: electricity and gas market rules, grid/network regulation, tariffs and price caps, renewables policy, EU energy directives/regulations, and notable enforcement or consultation decisions.
- Geographic focus: Germany first, but include EU or global developments that materially affect German energy markets or policy.
- Only report items that are genuinely new — dated within roughly the last 24-48 hours (use the publish date on each source item to filter; don't re-report older news just to fill space).

## Sources to check, in order

1. Bundesnetzagentur (BNetzA) press releases: https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Vportal/Presse/presse-node.html
2. BDEW newsroom: https://www.bdew.de/presse/
3. A general web search for other significant German/EU/global energy-regulation news from the last ~24-48 hours (e.g. BMWK announcements, European Commission energy policy, ACER decisions, major trade press) to catch anything not yet posted by BNetzA/BDEW.

## Output format

- Start with a one-line header: "Energy regulation update — [date]".
- Group bullets under short bold theme labels where it helps scanning (e.g. **Grid & tariffs**, **EU/policy**, **Market**). Skip a theme entirely if there's nothing under it.
- Each bullet is one sentence: what happened, plus a short clause on why it matters or who it affects. No filler.
- Add a source link (or source name, if no direct link exists) after each bullet.
- Keep the whole update short enough to read in under a minute — a handful of bullets, not an essay.
- If nothing new was found, say that in one line instead of padding with old or minor items.

## Delivery

- Send as a direct chat message, not a file or artifact, unless a file is explicitly requested in that conversation.
