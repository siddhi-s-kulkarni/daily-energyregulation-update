# Daily energy regulation update

The energy industry in Germany is changing rapidly and so is the respective regulation. Staying on top of regulation that is affecting the technology, projects and ultimately the decisions taken by organisations is critical. Additionally, German energy regulation does not exist in a silo. It is created in conjunction with, and following, overarching European energy regulation requirements.

Therefore, in order to have current knowledge of this ever-changing energy regulatory landscape, this skill was built.

## What it does
- Specifies the scope of what regulation should be covered
- Suggests possible sources to retrieve data from with a signal to surface similar sources
- Specifies a consistent output format in which the results should be returned

The skill is modular by design. Scope, sources and output can be changed independently.
